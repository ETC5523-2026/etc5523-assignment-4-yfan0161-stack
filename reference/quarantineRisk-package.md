# quarantineRisk: Calculate Border Quarantine Breach Risk

An R package with an interactive Shiny app to explore how viral
transmissibility and vaccine efficacy affect border quarantine breach
risk, based on modelling results from Zachreson et al. (2022). It
includes the results as a dataset, functions to interpolate risk and to
find the minimum vaccine efficacy needed to reach a target risk, and a
Shiny app for public health students and policy analysts.
